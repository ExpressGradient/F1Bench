## Race character

Dry, ~26°C air, track ~43°C, 0% rain. Wind calmer than Friday’s 65 km/h quali gusts (race gusts ~43 km/h). Pirelli C3 hard / C4 medium / C5 soft; one-stop favoured, all three compounds live, hard may be ignored. Pit windows wide. 2026 active aero and energy deployment should help passing on the 2.2 km straight relative to older Baku, but the castle section remains a wall.

Unlike Madrid, this is not a processional prior. Baku combines real main-straight passing with a high genuine SC rate (Pirelli’s historical SC figure here is very high; model uses ~76% at least one SC/red rather than 89%, to avoid Madrid-style overfit).

## Grid vs call

Material departures: Piastri and Norris over Leclerc (McLaren race pace vs Ferrari, 0.001s quali gap between PIA and LEC); Norris and Hamilton over Hadjar (Red Bull launches + rust); Verstappen over Gasly (true pace, if the PU is even partly healthy); Antonelli from 16th to ~8th (Mercedes delta on a passing straight, SC-dependent for more). Track position holds from Gasly/Colapinto through the Williams/Audi/Cadillac/Aston group, with only small Haas/Racing Bulls swaps.

Antonelli himself said this is harder to overtake than Monza. The call is a points-to-P6 recovery if the car lasts, not a 19th-to-first repeat.
