# 2026 Spanish Grand Prix (MADRING) pre-race forecast

**Forecast cut-off:** 13 September 2026, approximately 07:30 UTC (race start 13:00 UTC).

## Evidence used

- The FIA provisional grid puts Norris, Antonelli, Verstappen, Hamilton, Leclerc, Russell, Piastri and Lawson in the first eight places. Sainz drops to P20 for impeding Bottas; Bearman and Stroll are permitted to start P21/P22; Stroll has a 40-place power-unit penalty. [FIA Document 57](https://www.fia.com/system/files/decision-document/2026_spanish_grand_prix_-_provisional_starting_grid.pdf) and [permission-to-start decision](https://www.fia.com/system/files/decision-document/2026_spanish_grand_prix_-_decision_-_permission_to_start.pdf).
- Norris beat Antonelli by only 0.011 seconds in Q3, with Verstappen 0.140s from pole and the first seven covered by 0.470s. Earlier phases and all three practices pointed more strongly toward Mercedes than Norris's exceptional final lap alone. [Official qualifying report](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/norris-denies-antonelli-pole-position-for-spanish-gp-in-closely-fought-qualifying-in-madrid.5AqJXaE8s1sLfv8dJVLqty).
- Friday's short, traffic-affected long runs made Russell the benchmark. Motorsport's compound/stint-adjusted analysis had Verstappen about 0.13s per lap behind, Antonelli 0.55s behind, the Ferraris around 0.9s behind and Piastri 1.51s behind. Norris had no representative high-fuel run after a gearbox problem. [Motorsport.com long-run analysis](https://www.motorsport.com/f1/news/mercedes-clearly-ahead-how-long-run-pace-compared-in-f1-spanish-gp-practice-/10854723/).
- The strategic evidence is unusually uncertain. Pirelli measured roughly 0.2–0.3s/lap degradation and front-left graining, but the green-flag pit loss is about 24 seconds and overtaking was extremely difficult in the support races. Medium-Hard is the natural one-stop; Medium-Hard-Hard remains competitive if Friday's degradation persists. [F1 team/Pirelli Saturday comments](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/what-the-teams-said-qualifying-in-spain-2026.014qyfNipmqu7dP0ZuCvfc) and [strategy analysis](https://www.the-race.com/formula-1/f1-teams-face-big-madring-strategy-dilemma/).
- The race is forecast to be hot, clear and dry, around 32°C ambient with a possible 53°C track temperature. [Motorsport.com weather preview](https://www.motorsport.com/f1/news/f1-spanish-gp-at-madring-sunday-schedule-weather-forecast-and-how-to-watch/10855127/).
- Post-qualifying market prices were used as an external calibration rather than as the model itself. At the cut-off, the liquid Polymarket mid-prices were approximately Antonelli 40%, Norris 35%, Verstappen 12%, Hamilton 6.5%, Russell 4.45%, Leclerc 2.9% and Piastri 1.7% before normalization; sportsbook prices showed a similar front-row concentration. [Polymarket event](https://polymarket.com/event/f1-spanish-grand-prix-winner-2026-09-13) and [post-qualifying odds snapshot](https://www.thespread.com/auto-racing/2026-f1-spanish-grand-prix-predictions-and-picks-our-best-bets-for-madring/).

## Method and interpretation

I combined the 13 completed 2026 Grands Prix, recent form, grid position, weekend pace and driver/team reliability in a Monte Carlo rank model. Because this is MADRING's first Grand Prix, outcomes were mixed across three latent race shapes: a track-position-led one-stop race, a high-degradation strategic race, and a Safety Car/red-flag-heavy race. Team-level pace uncertainty, starts, strategy errors, non-terminal incidents and correlated race conditions broaden the positional tails.

The resulting win probabilities are led by **Antonelli (37.89%)**, **Norris (34.51%)**, **Verstappen (11.38%)**, **Hamilton (6.27%)**, **Russell (4.34%)**, **Leclerc (3.03%)** and **Piastri (1.47%)**. Norris has the track-position advantage; Antonelli gets a narrow overall edge from Mercedes' high-fuel evidence and season-long strength.

Retirement risk is above a normal dry-race baseline because the circuit is wall-lined and unfamiliar, practice produced several heavy crashes, support racing produced a first-lap pile-up, and 2026 reliability has been poor. Stroll is the largest individual risk after repeated Honda failures and this weekend's fresh-PU water-pressure problem; Bearman also carries extra DNS/retirement risk while Haas rebuilds the car after his FP3 impact. The model averages about **4.82 race retirements**.

`retirement` is modeled separately from classification. A driver retiring late can still receive a numbered FIA classification; earlier retirements go to `nc`. `dns` includes failure to take the start, while `dsq` is a small separate post-event risk.
